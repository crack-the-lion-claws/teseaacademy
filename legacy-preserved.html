TESEA competence-based pedagogy

A repeatable lesson architecture designed for evidence of learning, not content consumption alone.

01

Engage & diagnose

Activate prior knowledge, expose misconceptions and frame the competency in a real-life context.

02

Explain & model

Short notes, diagrams, video, audio, examples and explicit success criteria support concept formation.

03

Practise, apply & prove

Retrieval, authentic tasks, quizzes, tests and exam-style questions produce mastery evidence.
